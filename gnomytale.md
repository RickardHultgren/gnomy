#Gnomy's Lantern and the Glade of Clarity

In a secret valley, hidden beyond all maps, lay the Glade of Clarity. Here the gnome Gnomy wandered about. He had a long snow-white beard that covered everything but his nose and a bright red cap that swung back and forth as he walked about the gardens in the valley. He always had a burlap sack and a storm lantern with him. "A proper garden should be full of winding paths and trees," argued Gnomy. "Every tree is like a story waiting to be written, rich in meaning."

"I have to go to the Old Tree! Otherwise it will come and get me! But where is it?" wailed Gnomy. "Oops!" The ground jumped so that the gnome almost fell over. "Oh dear!", he muttered and held the lantern up to his face. "Not only is the lantern low on fuel, now the roots are causing trouble too!" He sat down and began to feel his hand over the grass, which was orange from the sunset. "Ah, yes, I know you! I know what name suits you!" Gnomy put his hand on a root "Grow now!" said the elf. In the flickering light of the storm lantern, a thin tree sprouted from the ground and grew steadily. "Yes, you can grow!" said the tree with great urgency a moment grew so that it now had a substantial trunk. "If you let me name you, you can become a big tree". foliage towered over Gnomy The sun had just set "The flame is taking too much fuel!" grumbled the elf, turning down the light until it was just glowing. "Ha! I'll be fine for a while!"

He pulled out a rope ladder from his sack which even flew into the air. Gnomy climbed the ladder to the crown of the tree. "Let's see what treasures you have! Shimmer berries!" laughed Gnomy, as he saw the glow of the lantern reflected in the berries, "The comforting nut and pearls!" He carefully placed bunches of all four kinds of fruit in his burlap sack and then climbed down the ladder gathered himself and spontaneously jumped into the sack.

"What delightfully beautiful you're from here," he panted as he then looked up at the crown of the tree. He sat down on the ground and leaned against the tree trunk with the packed sack in front of him. "But how will I find the old tree?" Carefully he picked up a berry and laid it in the palm of his hand. "Shimmerberry…" he whispered as he inspected the berry in his hand and finally popped it into his mouth. Mmmm… has a sweet and sour taste, but changes a little each time you bite into them." Gnomy looked out over the landscape. "…that clump of roots over there, couldn't it make a beautiful tree?", he pondered. "Those roots are calm and unassuming. And the old tree is probably in that direction." He stood up and collected as much air as possible in his lungs. And then he blew a wind that swirled around all the leaves that had fallen to the ground. The leaves then singled down, neatly in a path that led to the root ball far away. But something rustled and moved. The gnome followed the path with his eyes. "Oh no!" he exclaimed. Halfway to the root ball at the end of the path, the hand could make out how leaves flew up and down in the gloom. "Some roots just mess up, and then I trip over them! It's too risky to go down that path. But what if I take another fruit?" and so he sat down again by the trunk of the tree and took a deep dark red plum from the sack. "Mmmm… Glow plum," Gnomy muttered with his mouth full. "Sweet, but a little bitter, like fine dark chocolate. Good for strength of character and inner strength". He looked out over the landscape again. "Yes, of course I can form a path leading back here, and more fruit will grow while I'm out for a little walk." Gnomy spat out the plum core, "But it might not matter much, since I'm already here." And then he took a nut out of the sack. "Lovely roasted, as always!" Gnomy drew the homely smell in. "The lovely hazelnut always grows dry-roasted." Gnomy chewed on the nut and looked at the scenery behind him, "Maybe I should go back on the path that led me here? But first I have to taste the last variety of fruit!" With great tenderness, Gnomy picked up a stalk with many small round beads on it. "Oh, last but not least, Glänse beads!" Gnomy put a small berry in his mouth "The taste of honey! Maybe there are great new opportunities to plant new groves somewhere far away?" Gnomy smacked contentedly after eating all the fruits. "Yes, each fruit brings new thoughts, but I still don't know how to proceed. Not knowing makes me so hungry! But at the same time, I've gotten so tired from the fruits I've eaten, so maybe I'll sleep on it after all!"

"Oh no! The lantern must be fueled!" Gnomy ran his fingers through his long beard. "I have to hurry!" muttered Gnomy grumpily. He plucked off a piece of resin. It glistened where it lay in the palm of the hand, illuminated by the glow of the lantern in the twilight. "Atta's wood chips!" With the help of his nails, the gnome removed the chip from the resin. "Worried" he then said in a firm yet soft voice. Staring at the resin, he waited, but nothing happened. "Hmmm… Sorry!" The cord crackled, and even hardened into amber. "Yes, that's how it was…" muttered Gnomy. "I was a little sad when I was up in a tree today. But now where is that end?" Gnomy reached for the notebook that the colossally long wick ran into. He opened the book and pulled even more wick out of the book. "Finally! Here is the end!" Gnomy pulled the wick through a small hole in the amber. The gnome brought the pearl along the entire length of the wick, into the lantern. The breathy, flickering light from the lantern came along even steadily.

Long after dark the beard was cleaned of resin that now sat like amber beads on the wick of the lantern, and the notebook was returned to the end of the wick cord. "Now the lantern should be able to burn for a while longer and the beard is clean!" Gnomy said, yawning. "But I still don't know which way to go next. Where is the old tree?" Then the wind whispered "No hurry, the Old Tree is patiently waiting for you." Gnomy sighed in relief "Phew, but then it doesn't really matter which way I go right now. I have a clean beard and a lantern that gives warmth and light. Maybe I should go to bed and rest?" With even it cracked next to Gnomy, who flinched and looked in the direction of the sound. And Gnomy burst out "Thank you Tree, It was a wonderful bed you have prepared!" The gnome bent down so that the top of his hat touched the ground. "I'll see which roots need to become trees tomorrow. And then I'll get to the Old Tree in time." The gnome settled down in the little bed of roots and fell asleep in the glow of the storm lantern.
